Four unfamiliar variations for v47

Status: prepared, not run with human readers. These optional tasks extend the historical six-task pilot; they do not replace its concealed v45/v46 card comparison. Give the prompts before the separate answer key and before running code. Record each initial prediction, explanation, time or hesitation point, and source used. After one targeted clarification, record the changed answer separately as assisted. Agreement with the author is not a score.

U1. Same twelve-step outcome, a storage boundary later

Two amounts start at three. Success adds one, failure subtracts one, and zero is absorbing. One store is unlimited; the other discards gains above fifteen. Can any twelve-update amount history distinguish them? At what first update could their amounts differ? Does the same finite-horizon result justify the same unlimited-time conclusion when independent failures remain possible?

Keep fixed the success probability within an episode and explain why a run of fifteen failures matters for the capped store. State what changes at success probability one. Distinguish a mathematical amount process from a physical learner maintaining its own apparatus.

U2. A zero response with an excluded site

On a periodic 32-site ring, rule 150 has a sixteen-update map equal to the identity. Two preparations initially differ at one site. A readout at update 32 counts differing sites other than that initially perturbed site. What does its zero mean? Are the two complete configurations identical? Could differences have spread at earlier times? Predict the external disagreement fraction at update 33 and explain which changed protocol made the response visible.

U3. Better labels, smaller deployment amount

Each strategy starts with six units before calibration. A labeled trial costs one unit and supplies no resource. Compare fixed purchases of three and five independent labels under the stationary two-orientation model, followed by twelve actions. Which purchase gives better orientation inference, and which leaves more resource for deployment? Could these rankings oppose one another? Name the fair no-purchase comparison.

Then change the question: make additional labels optional and free, keep the old policy available, and ask about the best achievable expected payoff. Can that optimum decrease merely because the extra observation is available? Do not answer by silently charging for it or forcing a particular policy.

U4. A metric, then a chosen update

A Bernoulli predictor has probability p strictly between zero and one. You are given only its Fisher metric coefficient 1/[p(1-p)]. Is a learning update determined? Now supply a fixed dataset with positive-label fraction a = 3/4, mean negative log likelihood, and continuous natural-gradient descent. Starting at p = 1/4, apply its exact finite-time map for optimization time log(2). Find the new p and explain why the mean loss cannot increase.

Finally distinguish that exact map from taking a straight Euler step in p or in log odds. Does correspondence of the continuous flows alone prove that those two finite straight steps agree? Optimization time here is not a physical clock.
