# Where to return

Eight questions for revisiting *How Matter Learns to Continue*. These links lead to worked examples and the existing meaning ledger; they add no pages or definitions to the book.

Use the covered PDF or the interior PDF, as labeled. Physical page numbers count from the first PDF page; printed labels are those on the book page. Place this companion folder beside the matching v46 PDF files. If your viewer ignores named destinations, enter the displayed physical page. The HTML also offers a local PDF chooser below for other folder layouts.

## 1. Did the examples change a response, or did the system retain an observation?

Compare parameter updates with the missing-frame state. A present activation, a retained observation and a learned response need different tests.

## 2. Can the same short description determine what happens next?

Use the coarse-state witness and the exact closure condition. Ask which output the description must preserve; do not treat every useful summary as an exact reduction.

- Chapter 23: one coarse state, different futures: printed 221; [covered PDF 226](../how-matter-learns-to-continue-v46.pdf#nameddest=figure.477) · [interior PDF 223](../how-matter-learns-to-continue-v46-interior.pdf#nameddest=figure.477).

## 3. Did the observations recover an exact value, or only a useful set?

The binary detector loses the amount ratio, but its reports still bound the loss rate. Keep the clock, initial amount, threshold and endpoint inequalities with the inference.

## 4. When does information change an action and its consequence?

Return to the declared action map, then trace calibration, retained setting, report, choice and resource update. Information alone does not construct or fuel the apparatus.

- Chapter 12: information and action: printed 154; [covered PDF 159](../how-matter-learns-to-continue-v46.pdf#nameddest=figure.344) · [interior PDF 156](../how-matter-learns-to-continue-v46-interior.pdf#nameddest=figure.344).

## 5. Am I observing a variable, or changing how it is assigned?

The lamp examples separate conditioning on an observed value from intervention. Carry the permitted operation and common outcome record into any comparison.

- Chapter 14: two causal accounts: printed 169; [covered PDF 174](../how-matter-learns-to-continue-v46.pdf#nameddest=figure.379) · [interior PDF 171](../how-matter-learns-to-continue-v46-interior.pdf#nameddest=figure.379).

## 6. Is the final amount enough, or must every earlier step pass?

Contrast the declining pool with the fluctuating resource walk. An episode that reached zero has ended; a later positive unconstrained sum cannot restore it.

- Chapter 30: the monotone pool: printed 272; [covered PDF 277](../how-matter-learns-to-continue-v46.pdf#nameddest=figure.561) · [interior PDF 274](../how-matter-learns-to-continue-v46-interior.pdf#nameddest=figure.561).
- Chapter 30 notes: positive at every boundary: printed 283; [covered PDF 288](../how-matter-learns-to-continue-v46.pdf#nameddest=section%2A.590) · [interior PDF 285](../how-matter-learns-to-continue-v46-interior.pdf#nameddest=section%2A.590).

## 7. Did the law change, or did its coordinates and readout change?

Carry states, preparations and observations through the recoding before comparing equations. A change of physical coupling is a separate comparison.

- Chapter 29: recoding a description: printed 263; [covered PDF 268](../how-matter-learns-to-continue-v46.pdf#nameddest=figure.547) · [interior PDF 265](../how-matter-learns-to-continue-v46-interior.pdf#nameddest=figure.547).

## 8. Does a compatible region tell me how probable it is?

A set of allowed preparations needs a weighting before it gives a probability. Keep this separate from an empirical claim about the prevalence of universes.

- Chapter 37: same region, different measures: printed 338; [covered PDF 343](../how-matter-learns-to-continue-v46.pdf#nameddest=figure.665) · [interior PDF 340](../how-matter-learns-to-continue-v46-interior.pdf#nameddest=figure.665).
