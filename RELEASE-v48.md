# Reader companion: v48

The first public release of the optional companion to *How Matter Learns to Continue* by Piotr Mikołajczyk.

Download `how-matter-learns-to-continue-v48-companion.zip` below, extract it, and start with its README. **The book PDF is not included.**

This is the already-validated v48 payload, preserved byte for byte. It contains 152 files: programs, frozen data, reader exercises, selected technical-note/figure evidence, and provenance. Its navigation guides refer to the v48 PDF, not to an unpublished later revision.

Six portable validation commands were rerun successfully before publication. Historical framework records retain their original dates and scope. Negative scientific results remain negative. See the repository's `VALIDATION.md` for what was and was not checked.
